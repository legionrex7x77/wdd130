Our Rafting Trips

Choose the rafting experience that best matches your age, experience level, and sense of adventure.

Family River Adventure

This trip is designed for families and beginners. The route includes calm water and small rapids, providing a safe and enjoyable introduction to rafting.

Canyon Rapids Experience

This trip combines beautiful canyon scenery with medium-level rapids. It is a good option for visitors who want more excitement without an advanced route.

Extreme Whitewater Challenge

This challenging trip is intended for experienced adventurers. Participants receive safety instruction before navigating powerful whitewater rapids.
